# Crisp Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

Crisp uses a quiet editorial interface for precise transcript-based media
editing: warm paper canvas, dark ink text, restrained green accents, and
monospaced operational labels. The interface should feel technical enough for
timestamp work but calm enough for long editing sessions.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#f7f7f3`
- Surface: `#fbfcf8`
- Side panel: `#f0f2ec`
- Ink: `#18201f`
- Secondary text: `#647069`
- Accent green: `#28724f`
- Soft accent: `#dcefe3`
- Border: `#dfe4dc`
- Warning/filler amber: `#c9821e`

## Typography

- Product UI: `Manrope`, heavy for display and medium for labels.
- Operational labels/timestamps: `DM Mono`.

## Motion

- Motion should be precise, short, and editorial.
- Preferred entrances: subtle y-shift, opacity, scale, or clip reveals.
- Avoid flashy neon, heavy gradients, exaggerated bounces, and generic SaaS
  blue/purple palettes.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use random colors outside the Crisp palette.
- Do not make the app look like a generic AI dashboard.
- Do not obscure transcript words or timestamps with decorative effects.
- Do not use jumpy transitions for calm editing workflows.
